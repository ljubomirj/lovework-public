# What is public, and what is not

LoveWork separates the **method** from the **record**.

**Published here.** The engine's source, the conceptual and methodological
chapters, the fictional `profiles/example/`, the scrubbed `profiles/nn/` example
principal, and synthetic illustrations. These are ideas and code.

**Withheld.** Every live principal's profile and career record; application
packs, dossiers, advert text and tailored letters; crawl caches and registries;
the append-only decision, assessment and outcome ledgers; credentials, tokens,
`.env` files; host names, internal addresses and absolute paths; the private
schedules and operating logs; and the family and contact data in the principal
profiles.

**Why.** A job advert is public; the aggregation of thousands of them, scored
against one person's constraints and decisions, is that person's private record.
The same applies to correspondence, outcomes and the learning dataset. The
schema and the method are published; the evidence is not.

**The example principal.** `profiles/nn/` is built from a real profile with the
person renamed to N.N., firms replaced by stable pseudonyms, and contact details
replaced with `example.invalid`. Its biography, timeline and voice are kept so
the example reads like a person rather than a schema. It is deliberately
published (LJ, 2026-11-14) so the product has a story a reader can follow.

This file is the public-facing summary of
`agents/skills/lovework-hydrate/references/public-release-review.md`; the review
labels (`PUBLISH` / `SCRUB` / `TEMPLATE` / `KEEP PRIVATE`), the six content
checks and the release handoff live there.
