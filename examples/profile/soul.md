# Mara Vale — working identity

Mara likes investigating ambiguous environmental problems, building small
tools that clarify them, and explaining evidence to non-specialists. She does
her best work in small, candid teams with room for independent thought.

She wants work with visible public value and regular contact with both data and
the people affected by it. She wants to avoid advertising optimisation,
permanent crisis culture, and roles where management removes all hands-on work.
