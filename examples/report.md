# Synthetic LoveWork report

> This demonstration uses the fictional profile **Mara Vale** and invented
> organisations. It contains no real crawl or candidate data.

## Pursue — Tideglass Cooperative: Climate Data Product Engineer

**Assessment: 8.6/10**

Strong overlap between Mara's geospatial work, public-facing environmental
projects, and the climate-intelligence branch. The small cooperative and direct
contact with towns fit her preferred working environment. Remote-within-Europe
also satisfies the hard location constraints.

**Uncertainty:** The advert does not say how much of the role is product
engineering versus client delivery. Clarify this before investing heavily.

**Suggested next action:** Read the team's published adaptation case study and
request a short conversation with the technical lead.

## Explore — Civic Evidence Lab: Environmental Investigations Fellow

**Assessment: 7.7/10**

This is not a conventional next job, but it is unusually aligned with Mara's
investigative-data possibility. Her experience explaining evidence to mixed
audiences is relevant. The fixed term makes it a reversible way to test a new
professional direction.

**Uncertainty:** London attendance and fellowship compensation may make the
opportunity impractical.

**Suggested next action:** Check the expected weekly attendance and funding
before preparing an application.

## Drop — AdSpark Metrics: Senior Attribution Analyst

**Assessment: 3.1/10**

Mara could probably perform much of the analytical work, but advertising
optimisation is an explicit avoid. Location convenience does not outweigh the
likely long-term mismatch.

**Suggested next action:** None. Preserve attention for better-aligned work.
