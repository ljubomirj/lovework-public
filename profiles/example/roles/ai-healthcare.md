# Alex Example — Role: AI in healthcare

> AI engineering at a healthcare company, hospital, biotech, or health-tech startup.

## What This Role Looks Like

An AI/ML engineering role at a healthcare company. Could be a hospital
building clinical decision support, a biotech using AI for drug discovery,
a health-tech startup building patient-facing tools, or a medical device
company with AI in their pipeline.

## What I'm Looking For

- **Substance**: Real AI/ML work applied to healthcare. Clinical NLP, medical
  imaging, drug discovery, patient-facing LLM tools, clinical decision support.
- **Role type**: AI Engineer, ML Engineer, Data Scientist, or Research Engineer.
- **Company type**: Hospitals, biotechs, health-tech startups, medical device
  companies, or pharma R&D divisions.
- **Location**: EU-based or global-remote.
- **Compensation**: Market rate, with equity.

## What Makes a Listing a GO

- AI Engineer at a health-tech startup with a clear product
- ML Engineer at a hospital building clinical tools
- Research Engineer at a biotech with AI in their pipeline
- AI Engineer at a medical imaging company

## What Makes a Listing a MAYBE

- "Data Scientist" at a healthcare company (unclear engineering scope)
- "AI Engineer" at a pharma R&D division (might be too research-heavy)

## What Makes a Listing a KILL

- Pure clinical research with no engineering
- "Data Analyst" at a hospital
- US-only or US-anchored
- Junior / entry-level
- Insurance company (not healthcare per se)
