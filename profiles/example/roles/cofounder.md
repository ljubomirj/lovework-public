# Alex Example — Role: Founding engineer

> Co-founding or first-hire role at an early-stage AI startup. Equity-heavy.

## What This Role Looks Like

A technical founding role at a pre-seed or seed-stage AI startup. I am
willing to take a haircut on cash compensation in exchange for meaningful
equity, because I believe in the mission and want to be all-in on the build.

## What I'm Looking For

- **Stage**: Pre-seed to Series A. I want to be early.
- **Mission**: AI/agentic/ML, applied to a domain I find interesting.
- **Equity**: Founding-tier equity, not 0.01% on a 100-person cap table.
- **Compensation**: Modest salary is fine. I have reserves.
- **Role**: Founding engineer or "first hire". I will write code, do research,
  talk to users, and help hire.
- **Location**: EU-based or global-remote.
- **Commitment**: Full-time, in it for the long haul.

## What Makes a Listing a GO

- "Founding engineer" / "Founding AI engineer"
- "First hire" at an AI research-lab-spinout
- Pre-seed AI startup with named founders and a clear thesis
- YC or equivalent batch with a strong technical team

## What Makes a Listing a MAYBE

- "Early engineer" without clear founding-equity framing
- Seed-stage startup with a clear product but vague equity
- "Research engineer" at a startup that could go either way

## What Makes a Listing a KILL

- Late-stage startup (Series B+) — not founding-track
- "Founding engineer" with no equity mentioned
- US-only or US-anchored
- Mission I don't believe in
- Junior / entry-level
