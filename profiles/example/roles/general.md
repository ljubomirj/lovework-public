# Alex Example — Role: General AI/ML engineering

> The default role type. Senior AI engineer at a product company.

## What This Role Looks Like

A standard AI/ML position where I do real engineering work. Building LLM
features, RAG pipelines, agentic systems, or production ML. Permanent or
long-term contract. Strong technical culture.

## What I'm Looking For

- **Substance**: Real AI/ML engineering. LLM features, RAG, agents, evals,
  or production ML systems.
- **Role type**: AI Engineer, ML Engineer, Senior Software Engineer (ML).
- **Company type**: Product companies with serious AI investment, AI-native
  startups, or research orgs with engineering culture.
- **Compensation**: Market rate, with equity.
- **Location**: EU-based or explicit global-remote. Berlin strongly preferred.
- **Team**: Senior engineers willing to mentor. Strong technical culture.
- **Work**: Own end-to-end systems. Not just "run the prompts someone else wrote".

## What Makes a Listing a GO

- Senior AI Engineer at a product company with real LLM features in production
- ML Engineer role with research-flavored work
- AI Engineer role building agentic systems
- Founding engineer at an early AI startup (if equity is meaningful)
- Senior Engineer with clear ML scope

## What Makes a Listing a MAYBE

- "Senior ML Engineer" at a non-AI company doing ML
- "Data Scientist" with strong engineering scope
- "AI Engineer" with mostly applied/MLOps focus

## What Makes a Listing a KILL

- Pure research with no engineering
- Pure SWE with no ML
- Junior / entry-level
- US-only or US-anchored
- Crypto / ad-tech / surveillance
- Process-heavy big-tech with no engineering autonomy
- Management / PM roles
