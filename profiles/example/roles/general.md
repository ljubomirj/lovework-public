# Alex — Role: General data science / ML engineering

> The default role type: an applied modelling or ML-engineering position at a
> company that acts on its models.

## What This Role Looks Like

A hands-on role owning a model or a pipeline end to end, in a team that ships.
Permanent, UK-based or UK-remote.

## What I Am Looking For

- **Substance**: a model in production, an evaluation harness, a real user.
- **Scope**: enough ownership to see a decision through.
- **Team**: reviewed work, honest measurement, no hero culture.

## What Counts Against A Role

- Dashboards as the main deliverable.
- A title that promises research and a job that maintains reports.
- Relocation outside the UK.
