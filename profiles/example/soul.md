# Alex — Soul

> The principal's *soul* — what they are, what they are looking for, what they want
> to avoid. Separate from the CV and the bio; the matcher scores against it.

## Identity

Alex Example. Based in Manchester, UK. Open to remote for UK companies and to
hybrid in Manchester or London; not open to relocation outside the UK.

**Stance.** A builder first: a working prototype and a measured result beat a
strategy deck. Skeptical of roles whose main output is coordination.

## What I Am

- Data scientist, six years in retail and insurance analytics.
- Moving deliberately toward ML engineering: pipelines, serving, monitoring.
- Comfortable owning a problem end to end — data, model, service, evaluation.

## What I Am Looking For

- **Work with a measurable outcome** — a model in production, a decision changed,
  a cost removed.
- **A team that reviews** — code review, design review, honest post-mortems.
- **Room to learn the engineering half** — serving, evaluation, data pipelines.

## What I Want To Avoid

- Roles that are 80% dashboard-building and stakeholder reporting.
- "Data scientist" titles that mean "SQL analyst with a Python notebook".
- On-call for a system nobody owns.

## Role Types

Data Scientist, ML Engineer, Applied Scientist, Analytics Engineer.
