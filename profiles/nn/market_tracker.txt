NN — AI-frontier market tracker (profiles/nn/market_tracker.txt)

The employers that are the players in the frontier-AI market NN's own profile
points at — post-training and inference providers, GPU/neocloud compute, robot
foundation-model labs, AI supply-chain and AI-data companies — a working list
for a weekly crawl, not a set of open adverts.
Compiled 2026-09-30 by the LoveWork agent.

Sources (all checked 2026-09-30):
  • Frontier List — "Post-training as a service"
    (frontierlist.com/post-training-as-a-service), picked by 46 people in AI.
  • Frontier List — "GPU providers for neolabs"
    (frontierlist.com/gpus-for-neolabs), picked by 52 people.
  • Frontier List — "Robotics labs and startups with the highest talent
    density" (frontierlist.com/robotics-talent-density), picked by 55 people.
  • Frontier List — "Neolabs with the highest talent density"
    (frontierlist.com/neolabs), 73 voters, $10B cap applied by the poll
    itself. Companies added 2026-10-03; careers URLs not yet enriched.
  • Frontier List — "Most interesting AI supply chain startups"
    (frontierlist.com/ai-supply-chain-startups) — poll of named insiders,
    $10b valuation or less.
  • Frontier List — "Most interesting AI data startups"
    (frontierlist.com/ai-data-startups) — poll of named insiders.
  • Frontier List — "Data companies for future founders"
    (frontierlist.com/data-companies-for-future-founders), picked by 52 people.
  • Breakout List (breakoutlist.com) — 92 startups picked by 15 investors;
    only the AI/ML, data, robotics, compute and frontier-tech rows are taken.
  • Pavlov's List (pavlovslist.com) — RL environments and training-data
    vendors, maintained by Chris Barber.
  • Pavlov's List — Robotics data (pavlovslist.com/robotics-data) —
    egocentric/teleoperation data vendors.
  • Live URL liveness checks only (anonymous curl, GET, following redirects,
    15s timeout, 2026-09-30). Every URL answered 2xx except the four marked
    "(unverified URL)", which are kept rather than dropped.

Format: • Name — URL — one line — [tags] (location if known)

Legend: POST-TRAIN = fine-tuning / RL post-training | INFERENCE = serving,
optimisation & inference platforms | COMPUTE = GPU/neocloud & datacenter
capacity | CHIP = custom silicon | ROBOTICS = robot foundation models & robot
data | DATA = training data, RL environments & evaluations | SUPPLY = AI
supply chain (power, optics, materials) | NEOLAB = frontier model lab |
OSS = open-source project/lab

Derived from — these are the AI-frontier company lists NN supplied, and they
fit NN because they are the post-training, inference, compute and
agentic-systems employers where an ML/AI researcher-engineer profile is the
hire: soul.md, "What I'm Looking For": "A role that lets me do real ML/AI
research or engineering, ideally at the frontier"; and its role type 4,
"General ML/AI research or engineering — the default. Research scientist,
research engineer, ML engineer, AI engineer at a frontier lab, research org,
or strong technical startup." market-position.md puts the strongest lane at
"applied ML engineering, agentic systems, research engineering ..., evaluation/
tooling/platform roles, data-centric ML", which is exactly what §A (post-
training/serving), §B (compute) and §E–§I (data, RL environments and evals)
hire for. Read with work_auth.md: NN is London-based, open to remote for
UK/EU-based companies or explicit global-remote policy, and NOT open to
US-only or US-timezone roles — most rows below are US (SF), so the UK/EU rows
(Harbor Partners Digital, Fractile, Umber Systems Group, Originator, Pine Networks Club's London office) and any
remote-first employer are the priority. soul.md's ideology exclusion applies:
Anthropic is excluded and does not appear here.

NOTES / caveats

- Vote counts come from the source pages as published (polls dated Aug–Sep 2026
  for the Frontier List pages, "as of September 2026" for the Breakout List);
  they will drift, so treat the ordering as indicative. The Frontier List
  pages also carry a second "also mentioned (one vote each)" table — those
  rows were left out except where they are the strongest signal (Harbor Partners Digital is not;
  it is a voted row).
- Four URLs do not answer 2xx to an anonymous curl: unsloth.ai and Fathom Research.com
  return 403 and physicalintelligence.company and prometheus.ai return 429
  (bot/rate limiting, repeatedly on 2026-09-30). They are kept and marked
  "(unverified URL)"; re-check before trusting a zero-result crawl.
- Anthropic appears in the data-companies list as a row ("Anthropic (data
  team)") and is deliberately NOT carried over: soul.md excludes it as an
  ideology-exclusion org. No Anthropic row belongs in this file.
- Mega-cap rows from the GPU and robotics lists were left out as low-signal
  crawl targets and a poor fit for soul.md's "small senior teams" / "no big-tech
  process-heavy environments (more than ~200 people in a team)" preference:
  Lumen Labs Labs Cloud, Oracle, AWS, Juniper Analytics Azure, Digital Realty (a datacenter REIT),
  Tesla and Amazon FAR. The 250+ rows that were kept (Lumen Group, Fathom Dynamics,
  Together, Willow Systems, Granite Group, Etched, Figure, Sable Partners, Handshake, Scale) are
  listed because the lists voted on them, but they will score lower for NN.
- The lists are US-heavy: of the rows here, only Harbor Partners Digital, Fractile, Umber Systems Group,
  Originator and Pine Networks Club's London office are UK/EU-anchored. work_auth.md rules
  out US-only and US-timezone roles, so the UK/EU rows and any employer with an
  explicit global-remote policy are the ones to prioritise; the rest are useful
  only where they hire through a UK/EU entity or employer-of-record.
- The "data companies for future founders" page names Fathom Research, Anthropic and
  Meta data teams as destinations; only the standalone data vendors are carried
  over here, because the mega-cap rows already cover that ground.
- Next step idea: the market_players source already crawls this file weekly —
  next, add an explicit "➟ Careers:" line to the highest-signal rows (§A
  post-training/serving, §B compute) and the UK/EU subset, and tag any advert
  that mentions post-training or RL, inference serving, evaluations/data
  pipelines, kernels/CUDA, or agentic systems.
