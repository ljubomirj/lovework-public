Philanthropies and science funders hire senior technical people for bounded,
high-autonomy terms of one to two years — deliver something specific, then hand
it over. That shape suits this principal (senior, pre-retirement). Live examples:

- **Renaissance Philanthropy** — Research Engineer, Speech AI/ML (applied 2026-09-07).
- **Openhome Foundation** — Senior Python Engineer (applied 2026-09-12).
- **Forecasting Research Institute** — research and forecasting roles.

**matcher signal:** GO for senior research-engineer / ML-engineer / technical-lead
roles at philanthropies, science funders, AI-for-science and forecasting

technical substance; DROP fundraising, comms, or grant-ops roles.

### (q) Think-tank and policy-innovation labs — design-your-own-role fellowships

Some think tanks fund a *person* rather than a post: a project-based fellowship
with a term and a budget, evaluated at the end for conversion to a permanent
role. **Cato's Innovation Project** ("Seed Round for Liberty") is the exemplar —
bring a project, get roughly two years and $100K+, deliver it, then convert.

**matcher signal:** GO for project-based fellowships and innovation-lab roles at
think tanks and policy organisations where the technical work is the point;
MAYBE for policy-research roles that would genuinely use the ML background;
DROP pure advocacy or media roles.

### (r) Consultancy first — contract AI/ML with a defined scope

Look for consultancy engagements rather than "doer" roles inside startups: a
defined deliverable, a bounded term, a senior rate (floor $10K/month, minimum one
month). The doing that would have gone into someone else's startup goes into

**matcher signal:** GO for contract/consulting AI/ML engagements with a defined
scope and a senior rate (3–6 months ideal); MAYBE for fractional or fixed-term

customer's local data. Process: collect + synthesise data → big LLM cleans →
train small dense / MoE-expert / LoRA → iterate. Target: local companies with
in-house data wanting efficiency gains (in-house text2sql, etc.). Marries model
building + local models + data collection + AI.

**matcher signal:** informational. A role building distillation / fine-tuning
infrastructure or data-centric ML platforms → bonus positive.
