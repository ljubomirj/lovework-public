# NN — Role: AI in finance / quant R&D bridge / data agents

> What this role looks like, in NN's words. Used by the lovework matcher
> when scoring job listings against this specific role type.

## What This Role Looks Like

This role is not an active return to the old quant career. Plain quant trading
and plain quant research are paused because that was the pre-2026 track NN wants
to leave. The only acceptable version is a bridge role: AI/ML is the primary
tool, and quant trading R&D or financial data is the domain where the AI/ML work
is applied.

## What I'm Looking For

- **Substance**: ML/AI is the primary tool, not an afterthought. If the role
  could be done as old-style quant research without modern AI/ML, down-rank it.
  LLM agents for financial data, alternative-data modelling, deep learning
  for forecasting, or research infrastructure for systematic strategies.
- **Role type**: ML/AI researcher in quant R&D, ML engineer in a trading
  firm, research engineer building data agents, or AI/ML lead at a fintech.
  Avoid plain quant researcher/trader roles.
- **Stage**: Either a hedge fund / trading firm with serious AI investment,
  or an AI-first fintech / data agent startup in finance.
- **Compensation**: Base + bonus standard for the role. Not looking for
  a haircut here — this is a serious position.
- **Location**: London strongly preferred, or UK/global-remote.

## What Makes a Listing a GO

- "AI/ML researcher" at a hedge fund or prop trading firm
- "Quant researcher, machine learning" with a clear AI focus
- Founding or senior role at an AI-finance startup
- Data-agent company working on financial data
- Alternative-data startup with ML at the core

## What Makes a Listing a MAYBE

- "Quant developer" with substantial ML/agent/data-platform exposure
- Mid-frequency trading firm adding genuine ML capability
- Traditional finance firm hiring its first ML researcher, if the role has
  enough agency to become an AI/ML bridge

## What Makes a Listing a KILL

- Pure execution trading (junior or senior) — I've done this
- Plain quant research — paused pre-2026 track
- "Quant" without any AI/ML substance
- US-only or US-anchored
- Prop firm with no research culture
- Junior / entry-level
