# NN — Role: Co-founding engineer at a startup (equity > pay)

> What this role looks like, in NN's words. Used by the lovework matcher
> when scoring job listings against this specific role type.

## What This Role Looks Like

A technical co-founder or founding engineer role at an early-stage AI startup.
I am willing to take a significant haircut on cash compensation in exchange
for meaningful equity, because I believe in the mission and want to be
all-in on the build.

## What I'm Looking For

- **Stage**: Pre-seed to Series A. I want to be early.
- **Mission**: Frontier AI, agentic systems, or AI applied to a domain
  I find genuinely interesting.
- **Equity**: Founding-tier equity, not 0.01% on a 100-person cap table.
- **Compensation**: Modest salary is fine. I am not looking for a salary
  to live on from day one; I have reserves.
- **Role**: Technical co-founder or founding engineer. I will write code,
  do research, hire, and talk to users.
- **Location**: UK or global-remote. No US-only.
- **Commitment**: Full-time, in it for the long haul.

## What Makes a Listing a GO

- "Founding engineer" / "Founding ML engineer"
- "Technical co-founder" with explicit equity mention
- "First hire" at a research-lab-spinout
- Pre-seed AI lab with named founders and a clear thesis
- YC or equivalent batch with a strong technical team

## What Makes a Listing a MAYBE

- "Early engineer" without clear founding-equity framing
- Seed-stage startup with a clear product but vague equity
- "Research engineer" at a startup that could go either way

## What Makes a Listing a KILL

- Late-stage startup (Series B+) — not founding-track
- "Founding engineer" with no equity mentioned
- US-only or US-anchored
- Mission I don't believe in (e.g., ad-tech, surveillance)
- Junior / entry-level
