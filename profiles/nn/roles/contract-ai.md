# NN — Role: Employee on short-term AI contract

> What this role looks like, in NN's words. Used by the lovework matcher
> when scoring job listings against this specific role type.

## What This Role Looks Like

A bounded engagement — typically 3 to 6 months — where I join a team to ship
something specific in AI. Could be:

- Building an MVP or prototype
- Leading a research spike
- Doing a focused build for a frontier lab
- Contract research at a startup

## What I'm Looking For

- **Duration**: 3–6 months, possibly extendable but with a clear end state.
- **Scope**: One focused outcome, well-defined. Not "ongoing engineering".
- **Substance**: Real ML/AI work. LLM reasoning, agentic systems, structured
  search, programmatic prompting (DSPy), or research infrastructure.
- **Compensation**: Day rate commensurate with experience, not equity.
- **Team**: Small, senior, technical. I am not a code monkey — I am a peer.
- **Remote**: UK or global-remote. No US-only.

## What Makes a Listing a GO

- Research-lab contract position
- Visiting researcher (3–6 month)
- Contract research engineer at a frontier AI lab
- Short-term fellowship with a specific deliverable
- MVP/prototype build for an AI startup
- Audit/review engagement with a clear deliverable

## What Makes a Listing a MAYBE

- "Open-ended contract" with no clear scope
- 6–12 month contract that could turn permanent
- Part-time consulting

## What Makes a Listing a KILL

- Permanent full-time (this isn't the role type)
- Pure SWE with no ML substance
- US-only or US-timezone
- Junior / entry-level
- Contract-to-hire with vague conversion terms
