# NN — Role: General ML/AI research or engineering

> The default role type. Research scientist, research engineer, ML engineer,
> or AI engineer at a frontier lab, research org, or strong technical startup.

## What This Role Looks Like

A standard ML/AI position where I do real research or engineering work.
Permanent or long-term contract. Strong technical culture.

## What I'm Looking For

- **Substance**: Real ML/AI work. LLM reasoning, agentic systems,
  structured search, programmatic prompting, RL, post-training, evaluation,
  research infrastructure, or applied ML.
- **Role type**: Research Scientist, Research Engineer, ML Engineer,
  AI Engineer, Member of Technical Staff, Founding Engineer.
- **Company type**: Frontier AI lab, AI research org, AI-native startup,
  or established company with serious AI investment.
- **Compensation**: Market rate, with equity.
- **Location**: UK or global-remote. Strong preference for London.
- **Team**: Small, senior, technical. I want to work with people I can learn from.

## What Makes a Listing a GO

- Research Scientist / Research Engineer at a frontier AI lab
- Member of Technical Staff at a strong AI startup
- ML Engineer role with research-flavored work
- AI Engineer role building agentic systems
- Founding engineer at an early AI lab (if equity is meaningful)
- Research residency or fellowship (even if it's a stepping stone)

## What Makes a Listing a MAYBE

- "Senior ML Engineer" at a non-AI company doing ML
- "Data Scientist" with strong ML substance
- "AI Engineer" with mostly applied/MLOps focus

## What Makes a Listing a KILL

- Pure SWE with no ML
- Data analyst / BI
- Pure quant execution
- US-only or US-anchored
- Junior / entry-level
- Process-heavy big-tech with no research autonomy
