## Decision Heuristics

When a job listing comes in, the matcher should:

- **GO** (8–10): strong fit on soul + role-specific criteria. NN should apply today.
- **MAYBE** (5–7): plausible fit, some misalignment. Worth a look if timing works.
- **FLAG** (3–4): low signal or partial misalignment. Only if nothing better.
- **KILL** (0–2): clearly wrong. Pure quant execution, US-only, data analyst, etc.

## Three Job Search Tracks (Jun 2026)

I maintain three parallel job search tracks. The LoveWork matcher should
score listings against whichever track fits best, but a listing that serves
multiple tracks gets bonus points.

### Track 1 — AI/ML RE Remote

Research engineer, ML engineer, or research scientist at a frontier AI lab,
AI-native startup, or strong remote-first company. Remote (UK/EU timezones).
The primary track. Covers `roles/general.md`.

### Track 2 — Paused: Quant Trading / Quant Research

The old quant trading / quant research track is paused. It was the pre-2026
career path and NN wants to get away from it. Do not surface ordinary quant
researcher, trader, portfolio-manager, or finance roles as positives.

Exception: a hedge fund, trading firm, or finance-data company role is acceptable
when the requirements are substantially AI/ML and the work is quant-trading R&D
only as a bridge toward ML/AI. Covers `roles/ai-finance.md`, but should be
scored conservatively unless AI/ML is central.

### Track 3 — Contract Work London

Short-term (3-6 month) contract work in London. AI/ML engineering, data
engineering, or data-centric roles. Can be at startups, trading firms, or
any London-based company that needs focused ML/AI delivery. Less picky
about company size/pedigree than Tracks 1-2. Key source: **Totaljobs**
(via Gmail NN-jobs alerts). Covers `roles/contract-ai.md`.

### Data-Centric Signal

Across all three tracks: data engineering, data curation, data-centric ML
roles should score alongside or above pure model-building roles. The data
is the unsexy part — but it's the most important part.
